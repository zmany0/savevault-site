Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Introduction

SaveVault ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our service.

2. Google API Services Usage

SaveVault's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. What Data We Access

SaveVault requires access to your Google Drive to function. Specifically, it requests the following permissions:

  • Read, write, create, and delete files in the specific directories it manages (specifically LocalBackups/{GameName}/).

We do not access, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive outside of the game save directories explicitly managed by the SaveVault application.

4. Data Storage and Transfer

All authentication tokens and local databases (SQLite) are stored strictly on your local machine. SaveVault operates completely locally and communicates only between your PC and Google's servers. We do not have our own backend servers, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your data to any third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the developer directly at: elzainfars@gmail.com or via the official repository.
